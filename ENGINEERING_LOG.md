### Added

### Security Lesson
Classical ciphers are useful for learning, but they are not secure enough to protect real information.

### Reflection
This lab showed me how easy simple encryption can be broken. Caesar only has 26 possible shifts, so brute force can quickly try all of them. I also learned that frequency patterns can still reveal information about the original message.
